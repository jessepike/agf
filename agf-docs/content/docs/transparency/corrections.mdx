Content pages say what is true now. This page records what they used to say and what was corrected. The full history is in the [changelog on GitHub](https://github.com/jessepike/agf/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

## 2026-11-10

### Positioning

- AGF now describes itself as a practitioner's map across the agentic governance sources, with proposals for the gaps (decision D12). Earlier pages called AGF an "architectural substrate" at "Layer 0" of a "seven-layer stack". That wording is retired. AGF is the guide across the layers and is not counted as one.
- Two of the four verbs were renamed. "Unifies" is now "Organizes". "Prescribes" is now "Recommends". AGF prescribes nothing a reader must conform to.
- The site's front door is now [Start Here](/docs/overview/start-here).

### What ships today

- AGF ships one machine-readable schema, the [Governance Decision Record](/docs/reference/governance-decision-record). Machine-readable forms for the other primitives are planned and have no committed date. See the [Roadmap](/docs/transparency/roadmap).

### Facts corrected

- **EU AI Act.** High-risk duties apply from 2 December 2027 (Annex III) and 2 August 2028 (Annex I) after Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. Serious incident reporting is Article 73. Earlier pages cited older dates and Article 62.
- **CSA Agentic Trust Framework.** The public version is v0.9.1, a review draft with one named author. Earlier pages said v1.0 and "co-developed with Microsoft".
- **Microsoft Agent Governance Toolkit.** Its README lists a self-assessed mapping to the ATF elements. Earlier pages called this formal conformance.
- **CSA AI Controls Matrix.** Version 1.1 was released on 22 June 2026. Earlier pages said July.
- **CSA MAESTRO.** Version 2.0 is still a peer review draft.
- **MITRE ATLAS.** Counts were updated to those shown on the ATLAS site.
- **OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions.** Still marked Development. They moved to their own repository in June 2026.
- **Model Context Protocol.** The 2026-07-28 version removed sessions. Earlier pages described session features.
- **Agentic AI Foundation.** Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and AWS are platinum members. Earlier pages said gold.
- **NIST.** The heading of the center's page reads CAISSI. Other NIST pages still use CAISI. The SP 800-53 overlay project was renamed COSIS on 2026-10-20.
